My Top Three Movies O.A.T

Soorurai Potru

Soorarai Potru is a 2020 Indian Tamil-language drama film directed by Sudha Kongara and produced by Suriya and Guneet Monga. The film stars Suriya, Aparna Balamurali, and Paresh Rawal. It is loosely based on the life of Air Deccan founder G. R. Gopinath.

Vikram

Vikram is a 2022 Indian Tamil-language action thriller film directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj and produced by Kamal Haasan. The film stars Kamal Haasan, Vijay Sethupathi, and Fahadh Faasil. It is the third installment in the Lokesh Cinematic Universe, following Kaithi (2019) and Master (2021).

Jai Bhim

Jai Bhim is a 2021 Indian Tamil-language legal drama film directed by T. J. Gnanavel and produced by Suriya. The film stars Suriya, Lijomol Jose, and Manikandan. It is based on the true story of a Dalit man who was falsely accused of a crime.
